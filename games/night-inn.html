Night Inn

Night Inn is a first-person psychological horror game where the player works a hotel reception shift, checks in strange guests, follows supernatural rules, and survives entities that use fear, mimicry, and panic against them.

The project focuses on pressure over spectacle: reading rules carefully, choosing when to trust what appears at the desk, and staying calm while the hotel turns routine guest service into a survival problem.

VizualTruth is building Night Inn toward a public demo target. Final release details, platform links, screenshots, trailers, and store pages will be added as the project moves closer to public release.

Genre Horror
