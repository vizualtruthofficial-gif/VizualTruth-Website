Swallowed Whole

Night Inn: Swallowed Whole is a placeholder multiplayer continuation of the Night Inn idea. The familiar building becomes a shared threat: players coordinate under pressure, manage scarce information, and try to survive a place that reacts to the group.

The design angle is cooperative tension. One player may hear something the others do not. One path may close while another opens. Choices ripple across the team, turning communication into both a tool and a liability.
